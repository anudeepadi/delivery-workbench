Delivery Workbench · standalone demo

Sprint capacity, worked out in the open

Enter your team's sprint and get the available hours, a commitment ceiling and a load check. Every number shows the formula behind it.

Runs entirely in this browser. No server, account, AI service or company data. The full app (React, Express, SQLite) is a separate project.

The example is an illustrative team, not client data.

Calculation

  1. Available hours — people × days × hours per day × focus %
  2. Commitment ceiling — available × 90%
  3. Planned load — planned ÷ available
